# Obsidian Sync Protocol

Goal: Keep Research OS and Obsidian vault aligned.

Strategy:
1. Research OS is source of truth for structured objects
2. Obsidian is human interface for reading and lightweight editing
3. Sync direction: Research OS -> Obsidian for structure
4. Sync direction: Obsidian -> Research OS for new notes
5. Conflict resolution: timestamp-based, human review for contradictions
