# Epistemology

## Epistemic States

FACT, OBSERVATION, EVIDENCE, CLAIM, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS,
SPECULATION, PREDICTION, UNKNOWN, DISPUTED, FALSE, OUTDATED

## Confidence

0.00-0.20 Very weak
0.20-0.40 Weak
0.40-0.60 Uncertain
0.60-0.80 Strong
0.80-0.95 Very strong
0.95-1.00 Exceptional

## Burden of Proof

Extraordinary claims require stronger evidence.

## Falsification

Every major hypothesis must define what evidence would cause it to be rejected.
