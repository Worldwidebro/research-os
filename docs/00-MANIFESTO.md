# Research OS Manifesto

We do not collect information. We build knowledge.

We unify all tools: VEX, Obsidian, Apple Notes, Notebook LM, agents, databases.

We do not trust claims without provenance.

We distinguish facts from interpretations.

We preserve uncertainty.

We actively search for contradictory evidence.

We attempt to falsify our strongest hypotheses.

We record failures.

We preserve research history.

We make experiments reproducible.

We version knowledge across all systems.

We measure our agents.

We treat questions as first-class objects.

We optimize for understanding, not volume.

We prefer primary sources.

We distinguish correlation from causation.

We document what we do not know.

We continuously update our beliefs when evidence changes.

Every important conclusion should answer:

1. What do we believe?
2. Why do we believe it?
3. Where did the evidence come from?
4. How strong is the evidence?
5. What contradicts it?
6. What would change our mind?
7. What should we investigate next?
