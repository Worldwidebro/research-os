# How can agent research operations maintain provenance across GitHub, Neo4j, and Qdrant?

## Why It Matters

Agent-generated knowledge must be traceable and reproducible.

## Known

- Git provides commit-level provenance.
- Neo4j provides relationship provenance.

## Unknown

- Unified provenance chain across all three systems.
- Handling divergent state when systems drift.
