# How should multi-tool knowledge (Obsidian, Apple Notes, Notebook LM) be unified without data loss?

## Why It Matters

Knowledge fragmentation across tools reduces research velocity.

## Known

- Each tool has different data models and export formats.
- Manual sync is error-prone.

## Unknown

- Optimal sync frequency and directionality.
- Conflict resolution when tools diverge.
