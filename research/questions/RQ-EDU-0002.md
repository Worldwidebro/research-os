# What assessment methods most accurately measure competency-based learning outcomes?

## Why It Matters

Assessment drives learning behavior. Misaligned assessment produces misaligned outcomes.

## Known

- Multiple-choice tests favor recognition over recall.
- Performance assessment is expensive.

## Unknown

- Cost-effective alternatives to performance assessment.
- Validity of portfolio-based assessment at scale.
