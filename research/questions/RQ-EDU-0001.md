## Why It Matters

Workforce readiness depends on durable skill retention, not just test performance.

## Known

- Spaced repetition benefits declarative memory.
- Vocational training often uses massed practice.

## Unknown

- Optimal spacing intervals for procedural skills.
- Transfer to real-world job performance.
