# How does route consolidation affect delivery time variance in medical courier networks?

## Why It Matters

Delivery time variance drives client dissatisfaction and operational cost.

## Known

- Route optimization reduces mean delivery time.
- Variance reduction is less studied than mean reduction.

## Unknown

- Effect of dynamic routing on variance under demand spikes.
